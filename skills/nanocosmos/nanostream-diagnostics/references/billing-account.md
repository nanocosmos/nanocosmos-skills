## Billing and Account Inquiries

### When to Use
A customer asks about pricing, plan upgrades, usage limits, or billing-related questions.

### What Support Can Handle
- Explaining plan features and limits
- Checking current usage in the Dashboard
- Technical questions about stream quotas
- Directing to the appropriate sales contact

### What Requires the Sales/Billing Team
- Pricing negotiations
- Custom enterprise plans
- Invoice and payment questions
- Refund requests
- Contract modifications
- SSO/SAML setup

### Quotes and Upgrades
- Self-service package pricing: https://www.nanocosmos.net/pricing/
- For a tailored quote, sales needs: number of ingest streams, streaming volume, and number of concurrent players
- 24/7 support services with guaranteed reaction times are available as a commercial option — contact sales

### Billing Contact
- Email: sales@nanocosmos.net
- Pricing page: https://www.nanocosmos.net/pricing/
- Contact form: https://www.nanocosmos.net/contact
- Existing customers: Dashboard > Billing

### Account Access Issues

| Issue | Resolution |
|---|---|
| Forgot password | "Forgot Password" on login page |
| Account locked | Contact support with organization email |
| Add/remove team member | Account admin: Dashboard > Team |
| SSO/SAML | Enterprise feature — contact sales |
| Trial expiry | Contact sales to extend or upgrade |

### Customer Response Guidance
- Provide general pricing info and sales contact for commercial questions
- For usage questions, direct to Dashboard > Usage / Analytics
- For trial accounts, always offer to connect with sales

### Contact nanocosmos support when
- Account is locked due to a suspected platform issue (not a forgotten password)
