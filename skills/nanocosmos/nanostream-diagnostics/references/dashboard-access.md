## Dashboard Access Issues

### When to Use
A customer cannot log in to, or is missing features or streams within, the nanoStream Dashboard.

### Steps
1. Clear browser cache and cookies; try a hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R)
2. Try incognito/private browsing mode
3. Verify account permissions (team admin vs. member vs. read-only)
4. Check whether the stream was created under a different organization or account
5. For SSO/SAML issues: contact sales (enterprise feature)

### Customer Response Guidance
- Walk through cache/cookie clearing first (resolves most cases)
- For permission issues, direct to account admin
- For account lockout, ask for the organization email to look up the account

### Contact nanocosmos support when
- Dashboard is inaccessible across multiple browsers and network conditions (platform issue)
- Account locked despite correct credentials
